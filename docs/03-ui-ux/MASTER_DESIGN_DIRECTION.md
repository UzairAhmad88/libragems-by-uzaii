## 1. Creative Direction & Brand Positioning

### 1.1 Brand Essence
**Libra Gems & Trading Co., Ltd.** operates at the center of the international gemstone trade in Bang Rak, Bangkok. The company bridges the raw, geological wonder of the earth’s mineral deposits with the sophisticated demands of international gemstone dealers, lapidaries, fine jewellery houses, and private collectors.

The digital identity must reflect:
- **Quiet Luxury & Restraint:** The brand does not scream for attention with flashy neon accents or aggressive retail sales tactics. It speaks with the calm, measured authority of an established trading desk.
- **Geological Authenticity:** Honoring the organic, unaltered beauty of rough stones, mineral specimens, and precision-cut crystals.
- **Institutional Trust:** Building immediate credibility with international buyers through structured, verifiable gemological information, transparent treatment disclosures, and direct access to company principals.
- **Understated Minimalism:** Generous whitespace, refined classical typography, structured grid layouts, and meticulous attention to detail.

## 2. Visual Identity & 60/30/10 Composition Rules

The visual identity is governed by the classic **60/30/10 composition rule**, ensuring harmonious visual balance across all screen sizes:

### Primary Canvas – 60%: Warm Off-White / Natural Ivory
- **Base Canvas:** Soft Ivory (`#FAF8F2`) and Warm Beige (`#F3EFE5`).
- **Psychological Effect:** Evokes fine unbleached archival paper, geological parchment, and gallery display surfaces. It softens high-contrast digital eye fatigue and provides an organic background that accentuates the natural color saturation of gemstones.
- **Application:** Page background, section panels, card containers, reading backgrounds.

### Structural Authority – 30%: Forest Green & Midnight Shade
- **Core Tones:** Deep Forest Green (`#174C38`) and Midnight Shade (`#103729`).
- **Psychological Effect:** Conveys permanence, ancient earth origins, wealth, stability, and institutional dependability.
- **Application:** Header navigation, display headings, primary action buttons, dark hero sections, footer background, and strong structural borders.

### Prestige Accent – 10%: Muted Champagne
- **Accent Tone:** Muted Champagne Gold (`#B99B68`).
- **Psychological Effect:** Suggests prestige, precious metal craftsmanship, and curated quality without the vulgarity of reflective yellow gold or brass.
- **Application:** Subtle badge borders, active tab indicators, focus rings, fine dividers, and delicate icons.

## 3. Reference Aesthetics & Design Influences

The visual architecture draws inspiration from world-class editorial and natural-material institutions:
1. **European Gemological Catalogues & Auction Houses:** (Sotheby's, Christie's Magnificent Jewels, Gübelin, GIA monographs) – disciplined typography, large single-stone hero shots, rich metadata tables.
2. **High-End Architectural & Mineral Journals:** Generous margins, asymmetrical grid alignments, restrained serif headers, deep captions.
3. **Heritage International Trading Houses:** Formal, diplomatic tone, clear contact points, unhurried commercial engagement.

---

## 4. What Must Be Strictly Avoided

To preserve premium brand integrity, the following visual clichés and design antipatterns are strictly prohibited:
- ❌ **No generic AI-template layouts:** Avoid oversized, meaningless wave dividers, neon glowing borders, and floating 3D spheres.
- ❌ **No retail ecommerce tropes:** No "Add to Cart" buttons, no floating shopping bag badges, no countdown timers ("Only 2 hours left!"), no crossed-out retail discount prices.
- ❌ **No flash jewelry glitz:** No fake glitter or sparkling starburst animations overlaid on gemstone photographs.
- ❌ **No noisy gradients:** Backgrounds must remain flat, serene, and textured through typography and composition, not multi-color linear gradients.
- ❌ **No intrusive popups:** No exit-intent newsletters, coupon wheels, or automated bot chat overlays.

---

## 5. Architectural Tone of Voice & Copywriting Ethos

The written copy throughout the website must mirror the visual restraint:
- **Factual & Accurate:** We describe gemstones by their mineralogical variety, verified origin, crystal habits, and physical metrics.
- **Transparent:** If a gemstone's origin is based on trade consensus rather than an accredited lab memo, we state so with dignified clarity.
- **Respectful & Commercial:** Addressing an audience of knowledgeable gem dealers, lapidaries, jewellery designers, and serious collectors worldwide.
