# Project Scope

## Included in initial release
- Premium responsive public website
- Home, About, Gemstones, individual product pages, Quality & Sourcing, Contact, Blog index and article template
- Three initial gemstone landing pages
- WhatsApp enquiry links with product-specific prefilled messages
- Secure contact/enquiry form
- Email notification to the business
- Admin authentication
- Product create/read/update/archive workflow
- Product image management
- Enquiry listing and status management
- SEO metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs and structured data where appropriate
- Analytics and Search Console setup guidance
- Deployment and handover documentation

## Excluded unless separately approved
- Checkout, payments, shipping calculations and customer accounts
- Live inventory/ERP integration
- Multi-language content management
- Automated translations
- Customer reviews or ratings
- Complex CRM integrations

## Assumptions
- English is the initial website language.
- The company will provide real product images and approved product facts.
- The company will provide access to domain/DNS and the chosen hosting accounts.
- A staff member will own incoming enquiries and keep product information current.
