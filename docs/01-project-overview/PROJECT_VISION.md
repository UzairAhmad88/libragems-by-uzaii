# Project Vision

## Project name
LibraGems Website

## Vision
Create a premium, trustworthy, internationally accessible digital presence for Libra Gems & Trading Co., Ltd. that presents the business and its gemstone offerings clearly and generates qualified enquiries from potential buyers.

## Business model
Business portfolio and gemstone catalogue with enquiry-led conversion. The website does not initially sell online or process payments.

## Primary audiences
- Gemstone wholesalers and traders
- Jewellery and gemstone manufacturers
- Gemstone collectors and mineral buyers
- International buyers researching suppliers
- Existing business contacts seeking product information

## Initial product focus
- Commercial-quality rough Hessonite Garnet
- Commercial-quality Turquoise
- Afghanistan cut-stone Rubellite

## Main conversion actions
1. Send a WhatsApp enquiry about a specific gemstone.
2. Submit the website enquiry form.
3. Email or call the business using verified contact details.

## Success measures
- Search engines can crawl and index the intended pages.
- Product pages attract relevant organic search impressions.
- Visitors can understand the products and business quickly.
- Enquiry channels work reliably on mobile and desktop.
- Business staff can update products and respond to enquiries.
- The site meets agreed accessibility, security and performance standards.

## Out of scope for initial release
- Shopping cart and online checkout
- Payment gateway
- Automated gemstone valuation
- Unverified claims about origin, treatment, certification or ethical sourcing
- Multi-vendor marketplace
