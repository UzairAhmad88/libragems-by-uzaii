# SEO Master Plan

## Objective
Build relevant organic visibility for the company's actual gemstone products and business identity. Rankings cannot be guaranteed.

## Search intent
Prioritize commercial investigation and supplier-intent searches while supporting them with genuinely useful educational content.

## Initial keyword clusters (research required)
- Brand: LibraGems, Libra Gems & Trading Co Ltd
- Hessonite: rough Hessonite Garnet, Hessonite Garnet supplier, commercial-quality Hessonite Garnet
- Turquoise: commercial-quality Turquoise, rough Turquoise supplier, natural Turquoise stones
- Rubellite: Afghanistan Rubellite, Afghan cut Rubellite, Rubellite gemstone supplier
- Company: gemstone trading company, gemstone supplier Thailand (only if geographically accurate)

## Page mapping
- Home: brand + broad gemstone trading proposition.
- Hessonite page: rough Hessonite Garnet topic.
- Turquoise page: commercial-quality Turquoise topic.
- Rubellite page: Afghanistan cut-stone Rubellite topic.
- About: verified company identity and business information.
- Quality/Sourcing: transparent quality and sourcing practices.
- Insights: educational topics that support product pages.

## On-page checklist
- One clear, descriptive H1.
- Unique title and meta description.
- Useful original copy matching search intent.
- Descriptive URL slug.
- Descriptive image filenames and accurate alt text.
- Internal links to relevant product/contact pages.
- Breadcrumbs where appropriate.
- No keyword stuffing or repetitive boilerplate.

## Technical checklist
- HTTPS.
- Correct status codes and redirects.
- Canonical URLs.
- XML sitemap.
- robots.txt.
- Mobile-friendly layout.
- Fast, responsive images.
- Crawlable internal links.
- Structured data only where accurate and eligible.
- No accidental `noindex` on public pages.
- Staging environment blocked from indexing.

## Measurement
Track Search Console impressions, clicks, queries, indexed pages and page-level performance. Review GA4 engagement and enquiry conversions. Establish a baseline after launch and review monthly.

## Important
Do not create fake reviews, fake business locations, doorway pages, link schemes, fabricated expertise or unsupported product claims. Do not guarantee first position.
