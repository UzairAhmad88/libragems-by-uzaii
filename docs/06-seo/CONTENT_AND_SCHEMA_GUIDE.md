# Content and Structured Data Guide

## Content standards
- Write for gemstone buyers, not search engines alone.
- Include practical details that the company can verify.
- Explain quality terms rather than using vague superlatives.
- Distinguish general gemstone education from company-specific claims.
- Keep product pages meaningfully different.

## Structured data
Consider:
- Organization: only verified company name, URL, logo and contact information.
- BreadcrumbList: where breadcrumbs are displayed.
- Product: only when page content meets Google's current eligibility and policy requirements; use accurate properties and do not fabricate offers, prices, ratings or availability.
- Article: for genuine editorial articles with accurate author/date information.

Structured data does not guarantee rich results or rankings. Validate with Google's Rich Results Test and follow current Google Search Central documentation.
