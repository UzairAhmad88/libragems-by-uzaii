# Product Brief – Commercial-Quality Rough Hessonite Garnet

## Working product name
Commercial-Quality Rough Hessonite Garnet

## Category
Rough gemstone

## Content objective
Help buyers understand the available rough Hessonite Garnet material and enquire about the actual lot.

## Content to collect from the business
- Actual photographs of the available material
- Confirmed origin (if known and supportable)
- Available size range
- Weight / lot weight
- Colour range
- Quality description and how the business defines it
- Treatment information (if known)
- Current availability
- Any laboratory documentation

## SEO direction
Primary topic: rough Hessonite Garnet.
Supporting topics may include natural rough Hessonite Garnet, commercial-quality Hessonite Garnet and Hessonite Garnet supplier. Validate search demand and intent before finalizing.

## Publishing rule
Do not describe a specific lot as natural, untreated, certified, mine-direct or from a particular country unless the company can substantiate the claim.
