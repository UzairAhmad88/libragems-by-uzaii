# Product Brief – Commercial-Quality Turquoise

## Working product name
Commercial-Quality Turquoise

## Category
Rough gemstone / mineral

## Content objective
Present the actual Turquoise material and provide enough verified information for a buyer to make an enquiry.

## Content to collect
- Actual photographs
- Confirmed origin (if known)
- Colour and matrix appearance
- Form and size range
- Lot weight / quantity
- Quality terminology and grading basis
- Stabilization or other treatment status, if known
- Current availability
- Any laboratory documentation

## SEO direction
Primary topic: commercial-quality Turquoise.
Supporting topics: natural Turquoise supplier, rough Turquoise stones, Turquoise material for gemstone buyers. Validate search demand and intent.

## Publishing rule
Do not imply untreated or natural status, mine origin, rarity or certification without evidence.
