## 1. Principles of Gemstone Catalogue Integrity

In international wholesale gemstone commerce, publishing fabricated or unsubstantiated specifications destroys trading reputation, alienates repeat buyers, and violates trade-description laws in Thailand, the United States (FTC Jewelry Guides), and the European Union.

### Core Catalogue Mandates:
1. **Rule of Non-Fabrication:** Never invent or extrapolate carat weights, origins, treatments, lot sizes, or clarity grades. If a parameter is unconfirmed, it must be omitted or marked `"Inquire for lot verification"`.
2. **Absolute Treatment Disclosure:** Adhere to **CIBJO (The World Jewellery Confederation)** disclosure standards. Treatment status (untreated, stabilized, heated, irradiated) must be stated clearly on product landing pages.
3. **Authentic Photography Only:** Placeholder or stock imagery must NEVER be presented as company inventory. Live product detail pages must display actual photographs of the physical stones/lots currently held in Bangkok.
4. **Origin Substantiation Protocol:** Geographic origins (especially *"Afghanistan"* Rubellite) cannot be claimed without verifiable documentation (accredited laboratory report or documented direct-mine chain of custody).

### 2.1 Trade Characteristics & Buyer Expectations
Rough Hessonite Garnet is traded in wholesale lots for lapidary processing into cabochons, carved beads, tumbled chips, or commercial faceted gems. Commercial buyers evaluate rough Hessonite based on:
- **Color Saturation:** Golden-honey yellow to intense cinnamon-orange and reddish-brown (known in Sanskrit as *Gomed*).
- **Yield / Fracture Rate:** Cleanliness of the rough, frequency of internal tension fractures, and percentage of usable cuttable core.
- **Lot Uniformity:** Sizing distribution across the parcel.

### 2.2 Mandatory Data Requirements Prior to Publication:
- [ ] **Verified Geographic Origin:** Sri Lanka (Ceylon), India (Orissa/Rajasthan), Madagascar, Tanzania, or "Trade Acquisition".
- [ ] **Available Lot Weight:** Total available parcel weight (in grams or kilograms).
- [ ] **Size Classification:** Average piece size (e.g., 2–5 grams / 10–25 carats per piece) or sieve millimetric sizing (e.g., 8–15 mm rough pebbles).
- [ ] **Quality / Grade Specification:** Commercial grade (translucent with characteristic treacle swirl, minor surface fractures) vs. Cabochon grade vs. Facet-grade rough.
- [ ] **Treatment Status:** Untreated Natural (Garnets are virtually never treated; confirm natural status).
- [ ] **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** e.g., Minimum 500g or 1 kg parcel.
- [ ] **Photographic Views:**
  1. Overview photo of entire parcel in neutral daylight (5500K).
  2. Macro close-up of representative rough pieces showing transparency and color.
  3. Scale photo against a millimeter ruler or standard jeweler's scale.

```
  │ Crystal System                  │ Triclinic (Cryptocrystalline agg.)     │
  │ Mohs Hardness                   │ 5.0 – 6.0 (Varies with porosity)       │
  │ Specific Gravity                │ 2.60 – 2.90                            │
  │ Refractive Index                │ 1.610 – 1.650 (Aggregate)              │
  │ Typical Association             │ Secondary mineral in copper deposits   │
  └─────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Trade Characteristics & Buyer Expectations
Turquoise is one of the most chemically sensitive and widely enhanced gemstones in global commerce. In commercial gemstone trading, **treatment transparency is legally mandatory**:
- **Natural Untreated:** Porous, unenhanced natural material. Requires careful handling, susceptible to skin oils and cosmetics.
- **Stabilized (Zachery / Resin):** Impregnated with colorless polymer or sodium silicate to increase density, harden surface for lapidary grinding, and permanently preserve blue/green color.
- **Reconstituted / Block:** Crushed powder bonded with resin (Excluded from fine gemstone trading).

### 3.2 Mandatory Data Requirements Prior to Publication:
- [ ] **Physical Form:** Rough nodules (nuggets), vein slab rock, or pre-formed bead blanks.
- [ ] **Enhancement / Treatment Disclosure:**
  - `Natural Untreated` (requires lab verification), or
  - `Stabilized` (Polymer / Resin impregnated for lapidary durability).
- [ ] **Matrix Pattern Description:** Spiderweb matrix, brown limonite matrix, black chert matrix, or clear uniform matrix-free blue/green.
- [ ] **Color Tone:** Sky blue (Persian blue), robin's egg blue, teal-blue, or apple green.
- [ ] **Available Parcel Weight:** Total stock in kilograms or pounds.
- [ ] **Geographic Provenance:** Iran (Nishapur), USA (Sleeping Beauty, Kingman, Royston), China (Hubei), Mexico, or "Commercial Trade Blend".
- [ ] **Photographic Views:**
  1. Dry surface view showing natural luster and surface texture.
  2. Wetted or polished cross-section showing matrix depth and color consistency.
  3. Bulk parcel shot with scale reference.

## 4. Gemstone C: Afghanistan Cut-Stone Rubellite

```
                        RUBELLITE TOURMALINE (CUT STONE)
  ┌─────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Parameter                       │ Technical / Gemological Standard       │
  ├─────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Mineral Species                 │ Elbaite Tourmaline (Cyclosilicate)     │
  │ Chemical Formula                │ Na(Li,Al)3Al6(BO3)3Si6O18(OH)4         │
  │ Crystal System                  │ Trigonal                               │
  │ Mohs Hardness                   │ 7.0 – 7.5                              │
  │ Specific Gravity                │ 3.01 – 3.06                            │
  │ Refractive Index                │ 1.624 – 1.644 (Birefringence 0.020)    │
  │ Pleochroism                     │ Strong (Dark red / Light red-pink)     │
  └─────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.1 Trade Characteristics & Buyer Expectations
Rubellite is the red to purplish-red variety of Tourmaline that maintains its red hue under both natural daylight and incandescent warm illumination (distinguishing it from common pink tourmaline). Rubellite is a **Type III gemstone** under GIA clarity standards, meaning natural liquid feathers, trichites, and healed fractures are virtually always present.

### 4.2 Critical Origin Verification Requirement:
- **The "Afghanistan" Claim:** Afghanistan's Nuristan and Kunar provinces (notably the Paprok and Pech valleys) are legendary for producing world-class rubellite with exceptional saturation. 
- **Verification Rule:** The website **CANNOT** publish *"Afghanistan Rubellite"* as a definitive origin claim without either:
  1. An independent origin report from an accredited gemological institute (e.g., GIT, GIA, Lotus Gemology, AIGS, or Gübelin), OR
  2. Transparent disclosure on the product page stating: *"Trade Description: Rubellite Tourmaline – Believed to originate from Afghanistan pegmatite deposits per trade provenance; origin report available upon buyer request."*

### 4.3 Mandatory Data Requirements Prior to Publication:
- [ ] **Individual Stone Carat Weight:** Exact weight to two decimal places (e.g., `4.85 ct`).
- [ ] **Calibrated Dimensions ($mm$):** Length $\times$ Width $\times$ Depth in millimeters (measured via digital micrometer).
- [ ] **Cut Shape & Style:** Oval Brilliant, Cushion Mixed Cut, Emerald Cut, Pear Brilliant, etc.
- [ ] **Color Saturation & Tone:** Vivid purplish-red, deep pigeon-blood red, or intense pinkish-red.
- [ ] **Clarity Evaluation:** Eye-Clean, Slightly Included (Minor trichites not affecting brilliance), or Included.
- [ ] **Thermal / Radiation Treatment Disclosure:** Natural Untreated vs Heated vs Irradiated.
- [ ] **Laboratory Certificate:** Issuing institute, report number, date, and QR code verification link.
- [ ] **Photographic Views:**
  1. Face-up (table) high-resolution macro shot under D65 daylight lamp.
  2. Profile / side view showing girdle thickness and pavilion depth.
  3. Back (pavilion) view showing facet symmetry and culet condition.
  4. 360-degree high-definition video showing scintillation and pleochroic color shift.
