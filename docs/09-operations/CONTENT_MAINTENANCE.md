# Content and Maintenance Operations

## Product updates
- Review availability regularly.
- Remove or mark unavailable products promptly.
- Replace outdated photographs.
- Update specifications only after business verification.
- Keep page URLs stable where possible.

## Enquiry handling
- Assign an owner to monitor the inbox/dashboard.
- Define a response-time expectation internally.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary customer data.
- Close or archive enquiries according to the business retention policy.

## Monthly website review
- Check Search Console indexing and search queries.
- Review organic landing pages and enquiry conversions.
- Check broken links and forms.
- Update dependencies and security patches.
- Review backups and admin access.
- Publish useful, accurate content when there is something valuable to say.
